Sounds & Blending

Mengenal bunyi huruf, pembentukan huruf (formation), hingga blending kata CVC, CCVC, & CVCC.

Tricky Words & Dikte

Menguasai ejaan kata khusus (sight words) serta melatih menulis kata dan kalimat melalui dikte.

Guided Reading

Membaca buku cerita berjenjang (Usborne, A-Z Books, Readers) secara mandiri tanpa mengeja.

Active Speaking

Melatih keberanian berbicara Bahasa Inggris melalui proyek presentasi mandiri di tiap level.

Target & Kurikulum Belajar (Explorer 1–4)

Tahapan penguasaan membaca, menulis, dan berbicara Bahasa Inggris secara berjenjang.

1
Explorer 1

Letter Sound Group 1–2 & Basic Blending

🕒 Durasi: ± 6 Bulan

🎯 Poin Pembelajaran:

  • Sounds & Writing: Menguasai 12 bunyi huruf (Group 1-2: s, a, t, i, p, n, c, k, e, h, r, m, d), pembentukan huruf, serta blending kata 3-4 huruf (CVC, CCVC, CVCC). Membaca & menulis dikte 45 kata.
  • Tricky Words: Mengenal & membaca 16 tricky words (I, the, he, she, we, offer, an, a, me, was, be, to, do, all, go, my).
  • Reading & Speaking: Membaca buku cerita (Buku "Ants", "Vegetables", "My Body", Usborne "Pirate Pat") dan berbicara topik "Introducing Myself" tanpa panduan guru.
2
Explorer 2

Letter Sound Group 3 & Tricky Sentences

🕒 Durasi: ± 4 Bulan

🎯 Poin Pembelajaran:

  • Sounds & Writing: Menguasai 6 bunyi huruf tambahan (Group 3: g, o, u, l, f, b). Menulis dikte hingga 60 kata dan membaca kalimat berstruktur tricky words.
  • Tricky Words: Mengenal & membaca 11 tricky words tambahan (you, your, come, some, said, here, there, they, no, so).
  • Reading & Speaking: Membaca buku cerita berjenjang ("Duck Gets Stuck", "Help!", "My Dog", Usborne "The Dressing-Up Box") dan presentasi "My Favourite Toy" secara mandiri.
3
Explorer 3

Digraphs, Long Vowels & Sentence Dictation

🕒 Durasi: ± 8 Bulan

🎯 Poin Pembelajaran:

  • Sounds & Writing: Menguasai Group 4–7 (long vowels, digraphs, diphthongs: ai, oa, ie, ee, or, ng, oo, ch, sh, th, qu, ou, oi, er, ar, dll.). Menulis kalimat dikte tricky words lengkap.
  • Tricky Words: Penguasaan 24 tricky words lanjutan (one, by, only, old, like, have, live, give, little, down, why, where, who, because, want, dll.).
  • Reading & Speaking: Membaca buku cerita ("Mervin's Garden", "Helping", "Where is Cub?", Usborne "The Deep Dark Woods") dan berbicara topik "My Classroom".
4
Explorer 4

Advanced Phonics, Magic <e> & Sentence Making

🕒 Durasi: ± 6 Bulan

🎯 Poin Pembelajaran:

  • Sounds & Writing: Menguasai Short Vowels & Double Letters (ck, ll, bb), Magic <e> (o_e, a_e, i_e, u_e), Alternative Long Vowels (ay, ew, ow, igh), serta penulisan Capital Letters. Dikte 200 kata & 50 kalimat. Menyusun kalimat dari gambar.
  • Tricky Words: Penguasaan 24 tricky words tingkat lanjut (once, upon, always, also, eight, love, cover, father, mother, thought, people, through, dll.).
  • Reading & Speaking: Membaca buku cerita kompleks ("Picnic", "Hibernation", Usborne "Run, Rabbit, Run!") dan berbicara topik "My Favourite Subject".
