Target & Kurikulum Belajar (Smart 1–2)

Tahapan pengembangan logika matematika awal si kecil berbasis standar internasional.

1
Smart 1

Counting 1–20, Basic Operations & Concept of Days

🕒 Durasi: ± 6 Bulan

🎯 Poin Pembelajaran:

  • Number & Quantities: Number Recognition, Formation, Sequence & Ordering 1–20. Counting with objects.
  • Comparisons & Shapes: Bigger or Smaller (size), Shorter and Longer (length), More or Less (quantity). Shapes: Triangle, Square, Circle.
  • Operations: Addition & Subtraction 1–10 (dengan alat peraga & tanpa benda), serta penyelesaian Addition & Subtraction Word Problems.
  • Time Concept: Name & Order of the Days (Sunday–Saturday), pemahaman konsep Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow.
2
Smart 2

Number 21–50, Regrouping, Geometry, Time & Graphs

🕒 Durasi: ± 6 Bulan

🎯 Poin Pembelajaran:

  • Advanced Numbers & Operations: Number Recognition, Formation & Words 21–50. Konsep Tens and Ones (puluhan & satuan). 2- & 1-Digit / 2- & 2-Digit Addition & Subtraction (dengan teknik regrouping). Advanced Word Problems.
  • Comparisons & Ordinals: Comparing 2 sets of objects using signs (>, <, =). Ordinal Numbers (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th...).
  • Geometry & Measurement: Identifying & Properties of 2D Shapes. Non-Standard Measurement & membaca data Picture Graph.
  • Time & Calendar: Reading Time to the Hour (hour & minute hands / o'clock). Name & Order of the Months (January–December).
